Services

Apartment repairs, painting, flooring and maintenance. Your local Melbourne team for a home that feels its best.

Apartment repairs

Practical repairs for apartments across Melbourne’s CBD and inner suburbs, with a booked arrival window, clear pricing and photos of the completed work.

  • Repair plaster damage, sticking doors, locks and cabinet hardware
  • Fix dripping taps, toilet issues, shower sealant and grout
  • Repair blinds, shelving, balcony doors and window fittings
View details

Apartment painting

Refresh a room or the entire apartment with careful surface preparation and low odour paint. Furniture and floors are protected, and rooms are reset at the end of each day.

  • Repaint interiors, ceilings, doors and skirting boards
  • Prepare cracks, dents and water stains before painting
  • Match existing colours, add feature walls or touch up before moving out
View details

Apartment flooring

From a damaged board to a complete replacement, Nafu supplies and installs apartment flooring or fits materials you already own, coordinating building access and acoustic requirements.

  • Install timber, engineered wood, laminate, vinyl and hybrid floors
  • Replace damaged boards and repair tiles or grout
  • Fit acoustic underlay and trims, then remove the old flooring
View details

End of lease make good

Prepare an apartment for handover with coordinated patching, painting and repairs. Work is planned around the vacate date, with an itemised invoice and photos for the bond claim.

  • Remove wall fixings, patch holes and restore painted surfaces
  • Repair damaged doors, skirtings, tiles and flooring
  • Document each completed item with before and after photographs
View details

For property managers

Apartment maintenance for property managers and owners corporations, with agreed response windows and clear job reports. Monthly accounts are available for portfolios of more than ten apartments.

  • Arrange repairs, tenancy changeovers, painting and flooring work
  • Resolve common property defects and apartment hardware issues
  • Receive photo reports, condition notes and consolidated monthly invoicing
