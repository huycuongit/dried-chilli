Flooring installation & repairs

Service details

Apartment flooring supplied, installed and repaired. Building access and acoustic requirements handled by your local Melbourne team.

Benefits of our services

From a damaged board to a complete replacement, Nafu supplies and installs apartment flooring or fits materials you already own, coordinating building access and acoustic requirements.

  • Install timber, engineered wood, laminate, vinyl and hybrid floors
  • Replace damaged boards and repair tiles or grout
  • Fit acoustic underlay and trims, then remove the old flooring
  • Clear quotes and completion photos to keep you informed

Booking inspection

Send photos and details of the work. Our team will arrange a visit to confirm the scope.

Work planning

Agree on the price and timing before work begins, including access arrangements for your building.

Fix and install

Repairs and installation completed with care, followed by a clean space and photographs of the finished work.
